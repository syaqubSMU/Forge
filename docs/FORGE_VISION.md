# FORGE vision (internal, for the team)

This is a reminder for the team. It is **not** website copy. SIGNAL does not state it yet, and the SIGNAL navigation stays simple.

> **FORGE simplifies the researcher's experience, so managing research is easy, not cumbersome.**

SIGNAL is one of the projects under FORGE, and the first one: the hero tool.

## How the team uses it
A one-question test for any design, wording or feature decision on SIGNAL:

**Does this make managing research easier, or more cumbersome?**

If it adds a step, a setting, a screen, or a word the person has to decode, it needs a reason. If it removes one, that is the direction.

## What it does not change
- The AI and human boundaries in `docs/SIGNAL_BOUNDARIES.md` still apply. Easy never means skipping the person's decision.
- No claim on the pages that the product cannot support.
- The SIGNAL pages keep the current simple menu: Home, Find funding, About, Settings.
