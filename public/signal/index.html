Research development

Find the right funding signal

SIGNAL reads current funding announcements, screens them against a researcher's established work, and shows the sentence behind every claim. It builds a deadline calendar and drafts an alert. A person decides what goes out.

Start a matchWhat it is and is not

1. Describe the researcher

Enter a name, a project description or keywords, and confirm the established research areas. Nothing about a person is stored after the run.

2. SIGNAL screens live

It reads current announcements at run time, screens them against those areas, and quotes the text behind each judgment.

3. You decide

Review the evidence, check the calendar, edit the draft alert and send it yourself. SIGNAL never sends anything.

What you can rely on

Every claim carries its source

Each rating quotes the exact sentence it rests on, with a link and the date the page was read. A quote that cannot be found in its source is discarded, and an unsupported claim can never raise a rating.

Eligibility is never guessed

If the announcement does not state it, SIGNAL says “Needs verification” and leaves it to a person.

The rating is calculated, not asserted

The model reports factual observations with quotes. A published rubric turns those into High, Medium or Low. Every result shows which rule produced it.

Dates are computed, not written by a model

Deadlines, internal deadlines and the calendar are ordinary code. A missing deadline stays “Not stated”; it is never invented.

Runs are logged. Runs, inputs and calendars are logged for the pilot report, kept for 90 days, then archived. Only the administrator can see them.
