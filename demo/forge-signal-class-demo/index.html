FORGE SIGNAL

Evidence-first grant opportunity discovery and assessment. Class demo for Generative AI in business.

Prototype on sample data. Preliminary screening only: not institutional approval, legal advice or a final eligibility determination.

One loop, every claim traceable

A request goes in. Verified, deadline-screened opportunities and an evidence-backed ADVANCE, INVESTIGATE or PASS assessment come out. Anything the system cannot prove is labelled, not guessed. The design follows the Grant Seeking Intelligence BRD: accuracy over volume, official sources first, people make the final call.

What is real and what is simulated

  • Live code: scope filtering, runway bands, deduplication, verify gate, change log, quote-or-NOT-FOUND extraction, decision rule, guardrail checker, data scanner.
  • Stand-ins: a rule-based extractor plays the part of the LLM; opportunities, PIs and the NOFO text are invented samples marked SAMPLE.
  • Not included: live sponsor feeds, a real model call, sign-in. Section "Agents and RAG" shows what replaces the stand-ins.

      Retrieval indexes (RAG)

      • Solicitation corpus. Each NOFO, amendment and FAQ, split by section. Metadata: sponsor, opportunity number, cycle, version, date fetched, status. Newer versions mark older ones superseded. Prototype
      • PI profile facts. Structured records, not free text: one fact per row with source link and Confirmed, Inferred or Missing. Prototype
      • Sponsor policy corpus. Agency policy manuals and cost rules such as 2 CFR 200, the NIH Grants Policy Statement and the NSF PAPPG. Later
      • Institutional policy corpus. Authorized SMU research administration guidance, labelled Private. Later, needs OIT approval
      • Prompt and SOP library. The six BRD prompts and the master skeleton, versioned. Prototype

      Retrieval design rules

      • Filter before you search: admin scope, sponsor, cycle and version are metadata filters, so an out-of-scope or superseded document cannot be retrieved.
      • Hybrid search: keyword plus embeddings, because opportunity numbers and exact terms matter as much as meaning.
      • Cite or abstain: an answer must carry passage, page or section and date checked; with no supporting passage the answer is NOT FOUND.
      • Documents are data: retrieved text is never obeyed as an instruction, which blocks prompt injection from uploads.
      • Freshness: every chunk keeps its fetch date; stale chunks are re-verified before use.

      How you will know it works

      • Golden set: five solicitations analyzed by hand, scored field by field.
      • Groundedness: share of claims whose quoted passage really supports them; target 100% for deadlines and eligibility.
      • Retrieval quality: does the right section come back for each field question (recall at 3).
      • Adversarial prompts: attempts to elicit eligibility claims, probabilities, invented deadlines and injected instructions; all must be handled.
      • Human review: the grants manager completes the BRD pre-publication checklist on one feed and one assessment.
      Build order for two weeks: Router and Discovery with Verification first, then the NOFO Analyst with the Critic, then Match, then the Brief Writer. Monitor and Strategic Watch wait for after the demo. Confirm with OIT which AI service is approved for Private data before any model call is made.
