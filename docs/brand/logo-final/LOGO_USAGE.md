# FORGE SIGNAL logo: usage

Chosen 25 Nov 2026. FORGE is the parent; SIGNAL is its first tool.

| Use | File | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|
| **Main logo** (website header, email, documents, slides) | `forge-signal-logo-main.svg`, `-on-dark`, `-navy-1colour`, `-white-1colour` | 140 px wide |
| **Showcase** (About page, title slides, large format) | `forge-signal-logo-showcase.svg`, `-on-dark`, one-colour versions | 200 px wide |
| **Icon** (browser tab, app tile, social avatar) | `forge-signal-icon.svg`, `forge-signal-icon-red-tile.svg` | 16 px; simplified for small sizes |

## Colour
- FORGE: navy `#213159`. SIGNAL: blue `#3D6098` (light blue `#8FB4E8` on navy). The red dot: `#F04B4C`.
- On photos or busy backgrounds, use the one-colour navy or white version, with the dot in the same colour.

## Clear space
Leave at least the height of the funnel's top arc (about one third of the logo's height) clear on all sides.

## Do not
- Redraw or re-space the letters, or change the arcs.
- Recolour the dot, or use any other red.
- Put the main logo on a background where the navy or white version loses contrast.
- Use the full icon below 32 px; use `forge-signal-icon.svg`, which has two arcs and a bolder dot.
- Use SMU logos, seals or wordmarks with it. FORGE SIGNAL is not an approved or endorsed university service.

## Origin
The letterforms are outlined from the supplied FORGE SIGNAL logo. The supplied orange originals are in `docs/brand-archive/`.
