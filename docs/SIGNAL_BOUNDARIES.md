# SIGNAL boundaries: AI & human collaboration

Source: 2026 SMU Technology Showcase, "Amazing AI: Faculty & Staff", slide "AI & Human Collaboration". These boundaries govern every SIGNAL workflow and interface.

> Humans are essential for setting the oversight, validation of information and direction, ethical guardrails.

## Where AI helps most
AI excels at speed, volume, and first-pass analysis, synthesis where speed is of essence.

- **Rapid synthesis:** summarizes NOFOs, policies, reports, accreditation standards
- **Drafting & structuring:** first drafts of emails, summaries, tables, briefing materials
- **Pattern identification:** highlights themes in qualitative data
- **Consistency & standardization:** applies shared language across reports and communications
- **Targeted analysis:** supports opportunity-faculty matching and tailored outreach
- **Early-stage sensemaking:** helps frame questions and surface options quickly

## Where human judgment is required
To provide trust, context, and accountability.

- **Final interpretation & decisions:** strategic choices, prioritization, and trade-offs
- **Policy, eligibility & compliance:** grant rules, accreditation standards, legal/financial risk
- **Equity & ethical considerations:** assessing impact on underrepresented or vulnerable groups
- **Context & institutional knowledge:** culture, history, politics, and informal constraints
- **Relationship-based work:** faculty engagement, leadership communication, trust-building
- **Accountability & defensibility:** owning outcomes in audits, reviews, and public settings

## How SIGNAL enforces them

| Boundary | Where it shows up in SIGNAL |
|---|---|
| Rapid synthesis (AI) | NOFO to Fact Sheet extraction |
| Drafting & structuring (AI) | Draft chair email, PI digest, match table; always marked DRAFT |
| Targeted analysis (AI) | Two-way opportunity/faculty screening with quoted evidence |
| Final interpretation & decisions (human) | Rating is a screening result; RD confirms every High and Medium |
| Policy, eligibility & compliance (human) | Eligibility is never assumed; defaults to "Unclear: RD must verify"; High requires RD-confirmed Clear |
| Equity & ethical considerations (human) | RD reviews the outreach list for who is surfaced and who is missed before any alert; Low and unverified profiles are not silently dropped from RD's view |
| Context & institutional knowledge (human) | Limited-submission status, internal deadlines, politics: entered by RD, never inferred |
| Relationship-based work (human) | Nothing is sent by the system; RD edits and sends every message |
| Accountability & defensibility (human) | Every claim carries a quote, citation and retrieval date; the decision log records who confirmed what |

## Hard rules
1. SIGNAL never sends, submits, or commits anyone to anything.
2. SIGNAL never invents a deadline, eligibility rule, award amount, or faculty interest. Missing stays "Not stated."
3. A person owns every outward-facing message and every rating used for outreach.
4. No Restricted data (SMU Policy 8.6) is entered.
