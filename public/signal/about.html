About the project

What FORGE SIGNAL is, and what it is not

A lightweight, public grant discovery application for university research teams, built on clear sources, honest coverage and no invented data.

Try the Explorer← Back to home

FORGE SIGNAL v0.1 is a lightweight, public grant discovery application for university research teams. It indexes a limited, clearly disclosed set of U.S. federal funding opportunity records, makes them searchable and filterable, shows normalized details with links to the official announcement, and lets users save a shortlist in their browser. Its value is a fast, reliable discovery experience backed by clear provenance and freshness information.

Who it serves

Research administrators

Find opportunities to share with faculty using broad search, sponsor filters, deadlines and official links.

Faculty researchers

Discover opportunities by topic with readable details and a saved list.

Department administrators

Track upcoming deadlines and revisit a chosen shortlist.

Research development staff

Scan across sponsors and disciplines with multiple filters and visible provenance.

Core capabilities in v0.1

  • Discover and search: by title, synopsis, sponsor, opportunity number and program metadata.
  • Filter: sponsor, research area, status, funding instrument and deadline, with a default 30-day minimum lead time users can change or remove.
  • Inspect: structured details with unknown fields shown as "Not specified", plus a prominent link to the official announcement.
  • Save: save and remove opportunities with no account. Saves live in the browser's local storage, so they are specific to that browser and device.

Trust and data integrity

  • Primary source is the public Grants.gov opportunity API, called from the server, never from the browser.
  • Every record carries its source system, source ID, source link and last-verified time, or an explicit missing state.
  • SIGNAL never invents a deadline, award amount, eligibility or status. Missing values stay visibly missing.
  • A failed sync never overwrites valid cached data, and the last successful refresh time is always shown.

Coverage and limitations

SIGNAL currently indexes selected official federal sources. Always verify requirements in the sponsor announcement. It does not claim to list all federal funding opportunities.

NSF opportunities will be validated against NSF's official Funding Search using a test set of at least 20 active records. Where Grants.gov coverage has material gaps, SIGNAL will link out to NSF rather than imply complete automated coverage.

Planned architecture

A Next.js and React front end, a Python FastAPI backend, a PostgreSQL database with full-text search, and a scheduled Python worker that syncs and normalizes Grants.gov records while retaining the raw payloads. Browse the live demo on the home page; it uses fictional sample data only.
