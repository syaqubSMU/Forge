What FORGE SIGNAL does

  1. ReadsCurrent federal funding announcements.
  2. ScreensAgainst the research areas you confirm, quoting the sentence behind every rating.
  3. Hands overA deadline calendar and a draft alert. You decide what goes out.

The advantage

  • Proof, not scoresEvery rating shows the sentence it rests on, with a link to the sponsor page.
  • You stay in controlNothing is sent without a person. Eligibility is never guessed.
  • Dates you can trustDeadlines are computed by code. A missing date stays “Not stated”.
