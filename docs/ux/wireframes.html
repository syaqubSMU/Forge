W2 · Result card: summary first, evidence on request

Problem: each card shows up to six blocks (three quotes, each with its own source line) before the reader reaches the next card. Change: keep the decision-relevant summary visible and fold the evidence into one disclosure that names how many quotes it holds. The "AI proposes, a person decides" boundary stays visible in the card itself.
