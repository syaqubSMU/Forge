What FORGE SIGNAL does

  1. ReadsCurrent federal funding announcements.
  2. ChecksAgainst the research areas you confirm, quoting the sentence behind every rating.
  3. Hands overA deadline calendar and a draft alert. You decide what goes out.

The advantage

  • Proof, not scoresEvery rating shows the sentence it rests on, or says plainly that none was found. Each links to the sponsor page.
  • You stay in controlNothing is sent without a person. Eligibility is never guessed.
  • Dates worked out by codeNothing is guessed. A missing date stays “Not stated”.
