What happens during a search
  1. Current announcements are read from the sponsor API.
  2. They are filtered to your deadline window, in code.
  3. The full text of each candidate is read.
  4. Each is checked against the research areas you entered.
  5. Every quote is checked against its source; unsupported quotes are discarded.
  6. Ratings are calculated from the published rubric.
Runs are logged. Runs, inputs and calendars are logged for the preview report, kept for 90 days, then archived. Only the administrator can see them.
Public and private data only. Do not enter student records, health information, financial account details or anything else your institution classifies as restricted.
