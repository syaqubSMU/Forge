AI & automation

Intelligent help, with people in charge.

Automation prepares drafts, extractions and reminders. Humans review and decide. These capabilities are in development.

Award document intelligence

Read an award agreement and prepare a structured record: sponsor, award number, amount, dates, reporting requirements, special conditions and deliverables, each tied to its source page.

AI-assisted document extraction

Pull key fields from budgets, amendments and correspondence into a reviewable draft. Uncertain items are flagged, not guessed.

Funding discovery and PI matching

Surface funding opportunities and suggest matches to investigator interests for a person to evaluate.
